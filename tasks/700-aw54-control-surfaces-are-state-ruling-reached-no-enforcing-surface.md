Unlike the other two it is a RULING to land, not a defect to fix -- which is the clearest reason the cluster is three rows and not one. · **The ruling, in AW5-4's own words:** "Machine-read control surfaces are STATE. A line a reader consumes first-match (`Tally:`, read at `seat_refusals.py:558`) is updated in place; the prior value is preserved verbatim in the file's history section. Amendment-only discipline governs narrative records, not control surfaces." · **It resolves a real and recurring contradiction**, which is why it must reach a surface rather than stay in a close packet. This repo's amendment discipline is strong and correctly so: ADRs, transcripts, handoffs and audits are immutable, and CLAUDE.md section 5 rule 3 says supersede with a new file or an in-file marker, never edit in place. Applied to a line a MACHINE reads first-match, that discipline produces the opposite of its intent: appending the amended value below the original leaves the reader resolving the ORIGINAL, so the record is scrupulously honest and the control surface is wrong. AW5-4 draws the line between the two genres. · **Recorded in the close packet is not recorded.** A ruling that lives only in a batch close packet is read by whoever reads that packet, which is nobody after the batch.
