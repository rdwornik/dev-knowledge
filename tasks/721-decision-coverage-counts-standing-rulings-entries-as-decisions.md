- [#721] [P2][S] **`decision_coverage` does not count STANDING_RULINGS entries, so a ruling with no mechanism row is the one decision class the query cannot see** - Section C of `to-cc/DECLARE-WINDOW-RULES-2026-09-11.md`, the 2026-09-10/11 window's close acts, in the operator's own words: *"`decision_coverage` (X1-1) counts STANDING_RULINGS entries as decisions, so a ruling with no mechanism row is visible."* · **A clause against `[#692]`, filed as its own row on purpose.** `[#692]` is X1-1 and owns `decision_coverage` in full; its Done-when carries `AMEND-SESSION-PLAN-009` A9-1..A9-3 **verbatim**, and A9-1's decision population is *"an ADR with status Accepted, an intake ACCEPTED/RATIFIED, a transport DECLARE-/AMEND-"* — three classes, and `protocols/STANDING_RULINGS.md` is not among them. Appending this clause to `[#692]`'s body would edit a verbatim carriage, which is the one thing that row's Done-when cannot absorb without ceasing to be verbatim; so the clause is filed beside it rather than inside it, where it is also not lost in a large body. · **It is the gap the register makes, not a general one.** The register is where a ruling lands when it has no other landed home — that is the file's own stated purpose. A ruling carried there and nowhere else therefore passes every existing decision check by construction: it is not an ADR, not an intake, and its transport `DECLARE`/`AMEND` is discharged the moment the register entry exists. Counting register entries closes that hole, and **section AH is the first live witness**: ten operator rulings and four architect rulings landed 2026-09-11, of which AH-A3, AH-A5, AH-A6, AH-A10, AH-B1 and AH-B3 have **no implementing row at all** and would be invisible to `decision_coverage` as A9-1 currently scopes it. · **What "an entry" means is part of the work, not a detail to settle in passing:** the register is sectioned `A`..`AH` and entries within a section carry their own ids (`AH-A4`, `N-1`, `I-F3`), so the countable unit is the entry and not the section — a section that landed fourteen rulings must not discharge as one decision. The existing `landed:` predicate blocks (`scripts/validate_landing_predicate.py`) already parse this file at entry granularity and are the precedent for the unit. · Done when: `decision_coverage` counts `protocols/STANDING_RULINGS.md` entries as decisions at entry granularity, a register entry with neither an implementing row nor a written "no implementation required" disposition is named by the refusal, and one test FAILS before the counting is added and passes after — a RED-first witness per ADR-108 §B, with section AH's un-mechanized entries as the fixture · refs `to-cc/DECLARE-WINDOW-RULES-2026-09-11.md` section C row 2, `[#692]` (X1-1, the mechanism this clause binds; its A9-1 population is the line that needs the fourth class), `protocols/STANDING_RULINGS.md` section AH (the witness), `scripts/validate_landing_predicate.py` (entry-granularity precedent), `to-cc/AMEND-SESSION-PLAN-009.md` A9-1, `[#664]` (the FPG-1 `implements` edge A9-1 reads), ADR-108 §B · kill-candidates: none — `[#692]` owns the query and carries A9-1..A9-3 verbatim, which is precisely why this clause cannot be folded into it; `[#686]` owns three gate predicates that measure the wrong property and names no decision population; `[#664]` owns the graph the query reads, not what it counts · **Archived annotations:** `tasks/archive/721.md`
