- [#886] [P1][M] **An emergency change records its temporary debt as DATA with an owner and an expiry -- JOURNAL prose is invisible to a gate, and a MUST surface has no waiver path at all** - Operator order 2026-09-17, at the handoff cut. The 2026-09-17 emergency hook shutdown (merges `33246c0a`, `9af353f7`, owner `[#863]`) was recorded as *"COLLATERAL, TEMPORARY, NOT SETTLED"* in JOURNAL 2026-09-17 (j) and in `.claude/settings.json` comment keys -- prose, which no gate reads. The parity register was never told, so the pre-handoff preflight (`gen_handoff.py` row `ship_gate`) refused the cut on three `fleet_parity` hard-fails: `settings-precommit-arm`, `settings-stop-backpressure`, `settings-deny-and-point`, all `MUST-absent` at the hub. The gate refused for the right reason: it is the first surface that checks every declaration against reality at once. **MEASURED, the missing record type:** the one machine-readable time-boxed divergence record the repo has, `.methodology.yaml` `sanctioned_divergences` (component + reason + `expiry`, honored by `enforcement_coverage.validate_allowlist_entry`), cannot carry this debt. `fleet_parity._eval_row` emits `MUST-absent` and returns BEFORE any declaration is consulted, and ADR-102 refuses `waivable: true` on a MUST row (`fleet_parity.py` loader, "a necessary condition is never waivable"). So the class of surface an emergency is most likely to switch off -- a MUST enforcement organ -- is exactly the class with no temporary-debt record. The two available moves are both wrong: re-arming before `[#863]` repeats the wedge, and demoting the tier MUST->SHOULD turns a temporary state permanent by accident, with no expiry to bring it back. · Done when: (1) a temporary-debt record exists as DATA, one entry per affected surface, carrying the surface id, the owning OPEN row, the act that created it (merge sha), a reason, and an expiry date; an entry with no owner, a CLOSED owner, or a past expiry is refused, never silently honored; (2) `fleet_parity` (and `audit.py ship-gate`) reports a MUST surface covered by a live entry as a distinct DEBT verdict -- not `AT-PARITY`, not `PASS-declared`, never a waiver of the MUST -- that the handoff preflight can carry as named, owned debt while the tier stays MUST; RED-first witness: an expired entry re-reds the surface with no code change; (3) MOOT AS FILED, same day: the three 2026-09-17 surfaces were restored rather than recorded (the ab-808 measurement showed they were not among the nine wedging hooks; `.claude/settings.json` `//hooks-RESTORED-AND-DISABLED-INDIVIDUALLY-2026-09-17`), so the first entries through it are the next emergency's, and `[#888]` states the cause; (4) the emergency path is stated at its point of use: an emergency disable of any gated surface writes its debt entry in the same commit, and a JOURNAL line alone does not count as recording it · refs `.methodology.yaml`, `ecosystem/parity-surfaces.yaml` (`settings-precommit-arm`, `settings-stop-backpressure`, `settings-deny-and-point`), `scripts/fleet_parity.py` (`_eval_row`, `_row_waivable`, ADR-102 loader refusal), `scripts/enforcement_coverage.py` (`validate_allowlist_entry`), `scripts/gen_handoff.py` (`_row_ship_gate`), `.claude/settings.json` (`//hooks-ALL-DISABLED-2026-09-17`), JOURNAL 2026-09-17 (j), ADR-102, `[#863]`, `[#883]` · kill-candidates: none -- `[#863]` owns re-arming the hooks, this row owns how the time they are off is recorded;
