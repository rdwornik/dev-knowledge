- [#787] [P1][S] **The routing unit is the SESSION, not the turn — the prompt cache is per model, so per-turn switching loses money** — filed from `DIGEST-2026-09-15-unrecorded-findings.md` §3, whose conclusions existed in NO repo surface. **MEASURED, lane aa-2, 2026-09-15:** the prompt cache is keyed **per model**. Across six mixed-model transcripts on this host, every one, the turn following a model switch carries a cache-write of **29,751–379,585 tokens** against a same-session median of **614–1,759** — 48x to 391x — while its cache-read collapses to the ~20–25K shared prefix. **A model switch re-writes the whole context.** · **THE ARITHMETIC THAT FOLLOWS, and it inverts the obvious instruction:** at the integrator's measured mean context of **232,875 tokens/call**, one switch costs **USD 1.46** into Opus and **USD 0.58** into Sonnet, against **USD 0.0812** saved per turn moved. **Break-even is 25 consecutive cheap turns. A merge walk has about eight.** So `opusplan`, a mid-session `/model` toggle, and every "switch model per task" instruction are a NET LOSS at this context size — right at the session level, wrong at the turn level. · **The correct shape is TWO RESIDENT SESSIONS WITH A FILE BETWEEN THEM**, which is what lane aa-2 landed for the integrator seat (`200e1f9d`, branch `worktree-lane-aa-2-integrator-model-split`). · **Why this is a row and not just a note:** this seat wrote "model by lane kind, switch per task" instructions repeatedly before the measurement existed, and those instructions are still in circulation. The work is to make the SESSION the declared routing unit wherever routing is expressed — the seat-boot render, the lane-contract generator, and the routing doctrine — so no seat re-derives it and none writes a per-turn split again. · **Coupling, not duplication:** `[#789]` owns the seat-boot render that never emitted its split at all, and lane aa-12 owns ordered-model-equals-ran-model enforcement.
