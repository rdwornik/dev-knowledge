- [#680] [P2][S] **The handoff generator emits six bundle files from templates that name no owning row, so `[#664]` clause 2 refuses the commit of every architect cut** - **Witnessed 2026-09-10 while landing the window-close bundle; the cut generated cleanly and then could not be committed.** `scripts/gen_handoff.py` renders `FUNNEL_HEALTH.md` and the five seat pastes from `templates/handoff/seats/SEAT-BOOT-{dispatcher,filings,handoff,integrator,lane}.md.tmpl`, and **not one of those templates names a backlog id**. The graph therefore finds no `implements` edge from an OPEN row to any file rendered from them, and the `graph-task-coverage` pre-commit hook (`[#664]` clause 2) REFUSES: *"no `implements` edge from an OPEN row. Name this file in the row's body, or name the row `[#id]` in this file."* · **This is structural, not incidental to one cut.** The seat pastes are a CUT-TIME HOOK (INBOX-038 / `AMEND-BATCH-V-001` section 1) that fires on every architect bundle, so every future architect cut reproduces the refusal identically. The files it blocked for the 2026-09-10 bundle are `docs/handoffs/2026-09-10-dev-knowledge-architect/FUNNEL_HEALTH.md`, `docs/handoffs/2026-09-10-dev-knowledge-architect/SEAT-BOOT-dispatcher.md`, `docs/handoffs/2026-09-10-dev-knowledge-architect/SEAT-BOOT-filings.md`, `docs/handoffs/2026-09-10-dev-knowledge-architect/SEAT-BOOT-handoff.md`, `docs/handoffs/2026-09-10-dev-knowledge-architect/SEAT-BOOT-integrator.md` and `docs/handoffs/2026-09-10-dev-knowledge-architect/SEAT-BOOT-lane.md` - named here so this row is the edge that lets the bundle land, and so the next reader sees exactly which artifacts the gate refused. `docs/handoffs/2026-09-10-dev-knowledge-architect/SUPPLEMENT.md` is the seventh and is named for the same reason: it is the fixed 7-question schema, generated EMPTY by design, so it can cite no row until an operator fills it. `RESIDUAL.md`, `PROBES.md` and `PASTE_THIS.md` clear the gate on their own, because a filled residual cites open rows and the assembler folds those citations forward; `HANDOFF_BOOT.md` clears it only once its hand-authored Purpose happens to name a row, which is luck rather than mechanism - this cut's first draft did not, and was refused. **That asymmetry is the diagnosis:** files carrying hand-authored content earn their edge as a side effect of being written, and the purely generated ones cannot earn one at all. · **Why the fix belongs in the template rather than in each bundle**, stated because the tempting repair is the wrong one: a seat can always satisfy the gate by hand-editing the rendered file or by naming six dated paths in a row, and both decay - the dated paths are stale at the next cut, and a hand-edit is overwritten by the next `assemble_paste.py` run. The durable repair puts the claim in the surface the generator reads. · **Neither adjacent row absorbs it:** `[#664]` OWNS the coverage gate and is the organ doing the refusing, not the defect being refused; `[#679]` owns the return path from a ruling to the `QUESTION-*.md` it answers, a different mechanism on a different file class - though the two are the same SHAPE, which is the argument for fixing both at the source rather than per-instance. · Done when: a bundle rendered by `gen_handoff.py --mode architect` commits with **no** `graph-task-coverage` finding and **without** any per-bundle hand-edit or dated path being added to a row - whether by the seat templates naming their owning row, by the coverage gate learning that a `docs/handoffs/<slug>/` bundle is claimed by the row that authorizes the cut, or by a recorded ruling that generated bundle files are out of the gate's scope; and **one test FAILS when a freshly rendered bundle carries a file with no `implements` edge** - a RED-first witness per ADR-108 section B, proven to bite before the check makes it green; and the 2026-09-10 architect cut is the fixture · refs `scripts/gen_handoff.py` (the SEAT-BOOT cut-time hook, INBOX-038 / `AMEND-BATCH-V-001` section 1), `templates/handoff/seats/*.md.tmpl`, `scripts/file_purpose_graph.py`, the `graph-task-coverage` pre-commit hook, `protocols/HANDOFF_PROCESS.md` section 13, `[#664]`, `[#679]`, ADR-108 · kill-candidates: none - `[#664]` owns the gate that refuses and `[#679]` owns the disposition return path; no open row owns what the handoff generator emits, and neither `gen_handoff` row (`[#404]` execution-mode supplement leak, `[#643]` P11 carriage) touches task-coverage · source: filed 2026-09-10 by the window-close handoff seat, from a refusal it hit while committing its own cut
