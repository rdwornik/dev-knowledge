A requirement that lives in a paste is re-pasted or forgotten; a requirement that lives in a register with a named organ and a trip-test is either enforced or visibly a candidate. · **The register's shape is the honesty mechanism.** A `status: measured` entry MUST carry an `organ` and a `trip_test`, and the trip-test must FAIL when the requirement is violated — a `measured` entry whose trip-test passes unconditionally is a REFUSAL of the register, not a pass. Everything else is `status: candidate` and carries NO organ field, so the register can never name an enforcement it cannot prove.
