- [#883] [P1][M] **The commit gate was never priced -- a mechanism ships with a counter of what it caught, and a gate with no catch in its window is removed, not tuned** - Operator order 2026-09-17, batch AB close: this window's single lesson. The local commit gate grew to 34 hooks with no measurement of catches per cost. Measured cost: ~244 s mean commit wall; a single commit 9+ min as a detached process; the `[#761]` amendment commit ran 12:10-12:22 and was refused on journal-anchor tree lag, then landed in 25.9 s once the gate was stripped. The PreToolUse guards in front of it permitted 477 of 573 calls (lane ab-808) -- they enforced nothing 83% of the time. Merge `6e9f0bb8` kept only data-loss protection local (`audit-index-freshness`, `organ-index-freshness`, pre-push `block-unanchored-push`, `block-ff-push`) and moved 31 hooks to `stages: [manual]`, run report-only by conductor job `commit-gate`; merge `acf62f8e` landed `[#834]`'s new commit-msg hook at `manual` for the same reason. **WHAT WAS GIVEN UP IS UNKNOWN, and that is recorded, not implied away:** nobody measured what those 31 hooks caught, so the strip reduced cost by a measured amount and reduced enforcement by an UNMEASURED amount; measuring it is owed, and the conductor job's range runs are its first data. · Done when: (1) every hook, guard and gate carries a counter -- per window, the runs, the refusals, the refusals that were a real defect (a refusal followed by a fix to the refused content, distinguished from a retry, a sync or a bypass) and the wall seconds -- written by the mechanism itself to one ledger; (2) a report computes catches per cost per mechanism over a stated window, and names the mechanisms with zero real catches in it; (3) the 31 moved hooks are measured over their first 30 days of conductor range runs and each is dispositioned from that number -- returned to a local stage with its counter, kept in CI, or deleted -- none by argument; (4) a mechanism with no catch in its window is REMOVED, never retuned, and the removal cites its counter reading · refs `.pre-commit-config.yaml` header, `.github/workflows/conductor.yml` job `commit-gate`, `docs/audits/2026-09-16-technical-lane-ab-808-guard-timeout.md` (unmerged, branch `worktree-lane-ab-808-guard-timeout`), `[#808]`, `[#834]`, `[#761]` · kill-candidates: none -- `[#808]` bounds a hook's time, this row prices its worth · source: operator order 2026-09-17, integrator-AB close
