- [#717] [P1][S] **The generator-carried launch line omits `-Model`, so a sonnet contract dispatched by its own carried line silently runs at opus** - Second of AX7-3's three measured dispatch defects, each filed as its own FILE row with a RED-first test. Carried at `docs/intake/2026-09-11-tech-batch-x-roster.md` (intake 93) Part 5. · **The mechanism, and both halves are individually reasonable.** `dispatch_command` emits the slug, the contract file and `-Effort` -- and not `-Model`. `Start-DispatchLane`'s `-Model` parameter defaults to `opus`. Neither is wrong alone; together they mean a contract whose own `Model` row says `sonnet`, dispatched by the line that contract carries, runs at `opus`. · **The failure is silent, and silent in the expensive direction.** Nothing refuses and nothing warns; the lane produces work that is entirely plausible and simply cost several times what the contract declared. A cheaper-than-declared error would announce itself in quality; this one announces itself only in the bill, which is the number nobody was measuring. AX4-2 and AX5-1 both make per-lane model and cost a declared, recorded quantity -- this defect makes that declaration untrue at the moment of dispatch, defeating the measurement at its source. · **A contract that carries its own launch line is making a promise about how it will run.** The line exists so the operator can dispatch verbatim without re-deciding anything -- a lane contract's own words are that dispatch constants "ride the line without being re-decided". A line that drops the model silently re-decides the most expensive constant on it. · **AX7-5's standing consequence until this lands:** `-Model` must be passed EXPLICITLY on every dispatch, and "cheapest admitted" resolves to **sonnet** rather than haiku, because haiku carries no admission row in `ecosystem/provider-registry.yaml`. · **AX12-1 CARRIED INTO THIS ROW by lane `lane-x-716-dispatch-defects`, 2026-09-11 (the AW-2 / `[#680]` pattern -- a clause addressed to a row does not stay SAID).** **AX9-4 (exists-before-build) is DISCHARGED BY REUSE rather than by a quote excusing a build: this lane creates NO organ, script, hook or doc.** `graph_queries.py process-list --render` was run live on 2026-09-11 and returned `scripts/gen_lane_contract.py` (triggered by `.pre-commit-config.yaml`), `scripts/gen_handoff.py` (triggered by `scripts/assemble_paste.py`) and `scripts/worktree_seed.py` (no trigger; ON-DEMAND-BY-OPERATOR, invoked by /lane-boot) -- three existing processes that already answer all three needs, so every fix below lands INSIDE one of them. **AX4-1 (floor declaration) is reported, not filed:** no `tasks/*.md` row in the tree carries a `floor:` key today (measured: zero of the open rows), `ecosystem/parity-surfaces.yaml` carries no lane-contract or dispatch-defect surface, and AX4-1 names the `decision_coverage` lane (X1-1) as its owner and the parity registry as its carrier.
