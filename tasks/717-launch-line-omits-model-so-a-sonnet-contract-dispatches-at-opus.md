- [#717] [P1][S] **The generator-carried launch line omits `-Model`, so a sonnet contract dispatched by its own carried line silently runs at opus** - Second of AX7-3's three measured dispatch defects, each filed as its own FILE row with a RED-first test. Carried at `docs/intake/2026-09-11-tech-batch-x-roster.md` (intake 93) Part 5. · **The mechanism, and both halves are individually reasonable.** `dispatch_command` emits the slug, the contract file and `-Effort` -- and not `-Model`. `Start-DispatchLane`'s `-Model` parameter defaults to `opus`. Neither is wrong alone; together they mean a contract whose own `Model` row says `sonnet`, dispatched by the line that contract carries, runs at `opus`. · **The failure is silent, and silent in the expensive direction.** Nothing refuses and nothing warns; the lane produces work that is entirely plausible and simply cost several times what the contract declared. A cheaper-than-declared error would announce itself in quality; this one announces itself only in the bill, which is the number nobody was measuring. AX4-2 and AX5-1 both make per-lane model and cost a declared, recorded quantity -- this defect makes that declaration untrue at the moment of dispatch, defeating the measurement at its source. · **A contract that carries its own launch line is making a promise about how it will run.** The line exists so the operator can dispatch verbatim without re-deciding anything -- a lane contract's own words are that dispatch constants "ride the line without being re-decided". A line that drops the model silently re-decides the most expensive constant on it. · **AX7-5's standing consequence until this lands:** `-Model` must be passed EXPLICITLY on every dispatch, and "cheapest admitted" resolves to **sonnet** rather than haiku, because haiku carries no admission row in `ecosystem/provider-registry.yaml`. · Done when: the launch line is RENDERED FROM the contract's `Model` row rather than omitting it, and a test asserts the rendered line's model equals that row -- a RED-first witness that FAILS today against a sonnet contract whose carried line dispatches at opus · **Done-when carried from AX7-3's clause** ("the launch line is rendered from the contract's `Model` row, and a test asserts they match") · refs `docs/intake/2026-09-11-tech-batch-x-roster.md` (intake 93, Part 5 AX7-3 and AX7-5), `to-cc/AMEND-BATCH-X-ROSTER-007.md` AX7-3 and AX7-5, `to-cc/QUESTION-path-registry-2026-09-11.md` (fix 2 of the three), `scripts/gen_lane_contract.py`, `ecosystem/provider-registry.yaml` (where haiku has no admission row), `[#715]` (the path row these three ship first for), `[#716]` and `[#718]` (the other two defects), `[#691]` (provider routing as an unordered role set), `[#288]` (the model-identity guard for unattended runs) · kill-candidates: none -- `[#288]` owns detecting which model ANSWERED after the fact and would report this defect rather than prevent it; `[#691]` owns the routing table's admission shape, not the launch line's rendering · source: `to-cc/AMEND-BATCH-X-ROSTER-007.md` AX7-3, id assigned by the operator instruction of 2026-09-11, filed by lane `lane-x-000-batch-x-roster-lands`
