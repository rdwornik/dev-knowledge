- [#793] [P1][M] **A withdrawn lane's live worktree built a rival organ** - Batch AA dispatched `lane-aa-13-resource-lifecycle`, then WITHDREW its contract and superseded it with `LANE-aa-14-resource-lifecycle.md`, which records the withdrawal as *"verified at 0 commits"*. That verification was already false or was raced: `worktree-lane-aa-13-resource-lifecycle` carries TWO commits (`3da31370`, `01c446f7`) and 14 changed files. The two lanes overlap on TEN files and both create the same two organs - `scripts/resource_lifecycle.py` (aa-13: 575 lines; aa-14: 754) and `tests/test_resource_lifecycle.py` (464 vs 301) - plus both write `ecosystem/quality-requirements.yaml`, `.claude/settings.json`, `scripts/lane_cost.py` and `tests/test_quality_requirement_trips.py`. · **THE INTEGRATOR CANNOT RESOLVE THIS BY MERGING**, which is why it is a row rather than a merge decision: the two implementations conflict, so whichever merges SECOND either loses its organ to the first or overwrites it, and the winner would be decided by queue order rather than on the merits - silently, inside a conflict resolution. Batch AA integration therefore HELD aa-13 rather than merging it, and the hold is this row. · **THE WITHDRAWAL HAD NO MECHANISM BEHIND IT.** A contract is withdrawn in prose, in the superseding contract text; nothing tore down the withdrawn lane worktree, nothing signalled the running seat, and nothing refused its commits. A withdrawn contract whose lane keeps committing is indistinguishable at integration from a live one, and costs a full lane of work either way - here, 1,568 insertions that may be discarded. · **WHAT THE ID COLLISIONS SHOW ABOUT SCOPE.** The same batch produced FOUR task-id collisions (#787/#788 lane-vs-main, #790 aa-13-vs-aa-runtime, #792 aa-14-vs-aa-runtime) and TWO quality-register id collisions (QR-AVAIL-009, QR-RES-002), because six parallel lanes each allocated from a LOCAL MAXIMUM with no batch manifest to allocate centrally - and batch AA has no committed manifest at all, which ADR-110 requires AT DISPATCH. `[#788]` owns the allocator defect and `[#574]` owns manifest emission; this row owns only the withdrawn-lane half, and does not restate theirs · Done when: aa-13 has a RECORDED disposition (merged, or explicitly abandoned with its 1,568 insertions either salvaged into aa-14 or written off by a named ruling) so the refuse-to-finish checklist item "every lane branch merged-or-explicitly-abandoned" can close honestly;
