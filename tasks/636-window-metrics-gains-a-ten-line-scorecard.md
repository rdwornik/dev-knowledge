- [#636] [P2][M] **`window_metrics.py` gains a ten-line SCORECARD printed in every close packet and handoff** — **Filed 2026-09-05 by integrator ruling; candidate, filed not built.** The numbers a window is judged on already exist and are re-derived by hand every time, which is how they drift between a close packet and the handoff quoting it. The ten rows: rows closed/touched · hard-fail + WARN with trend · failing nodeids with baseline seconds · P1 pre-merge and regressions at merge · time-to-merge per lane · % of lanes on codespace · asks RED / re-asked · bundle bytes with the window-specific % · consumers at 0 FAIL · tokens by model class. **LIBRARY-FIRST IS THE BINDING CONSTRAINT:** extend the existing module, introduce NO new store. Every row is computable from surfaces the repo already maintains — the funnel detectors, the ship-gate, `git log`, the manifest and close packets — so a new datastore would be a second source of truth for numbers that already have one, which is the drift most of these gates exist to prevent. **Evidence it is needed, from the window that filed it:** the ship-gate headline was read four times across one window and moved 133 → 134 → 136 → 137, twice at a byte-identical tree, because roughly half its WARN lines are calendar-driven `doc_rot`. A scorecard printing scalars without separating what moved on the clock from what moved on the work reproduces that confusion at ten times the surface area — the trend column is the feature, not decoration. · Done when: `window_metrics.py` emits the ten named rows from existing surfaces with no new persisted store; the close-packet and handoff generators call it rather than restating numbers in prose; each row that can move without any work having happened carries its own trend/attribution; and a seeded test pins that a row whose ground truth is unavailable reports UNAVAILABLE rather than a plausible zero · refs `scripts/window_metrics.py`, `docs/audits/2026-08-26-technical-w2a-perf-core.md` (the dated-measurement convention), the ADR-111 funnel surfaces · kill-candidates: none — extends an existing module and closes nothing; the hand-filled scorecard in the 2026-09-05 close packet is the one-off this row replaces · source: integrator ruling 2026-09-05, merge-queue item 6
