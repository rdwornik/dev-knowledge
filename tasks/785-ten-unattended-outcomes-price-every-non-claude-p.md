- [#785] [P2][M] **Ten unattended outcomes price every non-Claude provider CLI on this box, and the metering unit is named where USD-per-token is the wrong instrument** - batch Z lane 4, frozen at `LANE-z-4-non-claude-execution.md` in the operator's prompts directory. **This row is the UNATTENDED half of a question `[#753]` deliberately cannot answer.** `[#753]` is frozen at `shape: interactive` - it reports after every task and must never run unattended - so it measures what a non-Claude CLI can do *with a supervisor*. Nothing measures what one can do with nobody watching, and that is the only mode a `--bg` lane, a nightly routine or conductor E can ever use. The two rows are complements, not rivals: `[#753]` decides ADMISSION as a supervised producer, this one decides whether a CLI is REACHABLE at all when no human can answer a prompt. · **The census the contract froze is REFUTED IN PART, and the correction is this row's first measured fact.** The contract declares `cursor-agent`, `aider`, `llm` and `amp` absent.
