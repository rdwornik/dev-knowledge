So this lane AUTHORS the rates rather than reading them, which is why it is the registry's SOLE OWNER for batch Y and `[#753]` is sequenced behind it. · **What exists now.** A `rate_card:` block declaring currency, unit, `as_of`, provenance and the two cache multipliers ONCE for the file; per-model `rates:` on the model rows, where a price belongs; `provider_registry.resolve_rate`, which re-reads the file on every call so an edit to the card moves every dollar figure the repo reports; and `scripts/lane_cost.py`, which reads the four token counts every assistant turn already carries in the session transcripts and prices them. `fleet_health` prints a `[cost]` line — money per batch and per model — from `logs/LANE-COSTS.jsonl` in one file read. · **Unpriced is not free, and that is the safety property the whole design rests on.** A model with no `rates:` row REFUSES by name (`RateUnavailable`) rather than costing at zero; its tokens are still counted and reported, and its money is never summed into a total, so a reader can always say how much of a figure is missing. A 0.0 rate would meet any budget spectacularly and mean nothing — the class `merge_receipt` already refuses for a 0.0-minute receipt. The five non-Anthropic ids stay unpriced honestly: grok's one measured datum is a whole-CALL price, not a per-token rate. · **Cache tokens are the bill.** Measured on this lane's own transcript: 21,308,461 cache-read tokens against 212 fresh input tokens. The multipliers are declared once on the card and applied in the reader; nothing in the cost module knows them. · **`logs/TOKEN-LOG.md` is RULED FED, not deleted** — see the ruling recorded in the end-of-lane artifact. It is ADR-29/ADR-39 append-only with eighteen months of history and deleting it destroys the only longitudinal usage record the repo has; what had actually failed was its FEEDING (hand-pasted `ccusage --json` weekly via `/session-summary`, last fed 2026-08-04). `lane_cost.py token-log --append` feeds it in its own established format, PREPENDING under the header and rewriting no existing byte, and the entry states its method because per-lane-with-cache and fleet-weekly-without-cache are not comparable line-for-line. · **What is NOT done, and why it is not an omission.** The cost row is its own append-only ledger joined onto the merge receipt by slug, rather than a field inside the receipt row: `scripts/merge_receipt.py` is `[#750]`'s SOLE-OWNED file for batch Y and `[#750]` is rewriting its completeness predicate concurrently, so opening it here would be an undeclared third claim.
