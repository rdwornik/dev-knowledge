- [#552] [P2][M] **Window-close disposition + archival routine — every new audit gets a disposition, every terminal ADR/intake is archived** — the mechanism that stops the pile-up recurring. Retirement in this repo is manual and unproposed: every archival move in its history was executed by hand (`216ce3a8` ADRs, `6551d363` intake under [#398], `f095a81f` intake #10), and the ADR leg of any status-keyed check is **structurally unreachable** — H3's bar (`docs/decisions/README.md`) keys on `Superseded` / `Deprecated`, values carried by **zero live ADRs**, because nothing ever writes them. Two legs, zero new organs. **(a) The ratchet:** an `archival_residency` check in the existing `scripts/audit_checks/` package, therefore already carried by the `audit-health` pre-commit gate, that FAILs a terminal-status document sitting outside its own `archive/` — scope `docs/intake/**` plus `docs/decisions/**`, with `docs/audits/**` and `docs/handoffs/**` EXCLUDED by the ADR-100 keep-all ruling and that exclusion stated in the check's own docstring so a future reader cannot mistake silence for an oversight. **(b) The proposer:** a section folded into the existing Tier-1 Stop hook (`propose_closures.py`, already detect-and-propose and never-mutating) naming undispositioned new audits, discharged ACCEPTED intakes with a drafted `consumed-by:` line, deferrals whose `trigger:` id has departed, and ADR-98 §6 survival-metric breaches.
