- [#790] [P1][M] **Runtime resource lifecycle, increment 1: a heap cap, a seat lifetime, a tree teardown and a transcript bound** - the harness has organs for decisions, files, tests, gates and process-as-workflow and **nothing owns runtime resources**, and every resource incident of the 2026-09 window is that single absence: orphaned process trees, OOM-killed commits, idle sessions at ~15 GB, lanes slowed by memory pressure, a transcript that freezes the client. `scripts/resource_lifecycle.py` is the first INCREMENT of that missing organ and deliberately **not** the organ itself -- who may allocate, for how long, what reclaims, and what refuses when the budget is gone is an architectural decision that belongs in the decision engine (intake -> alternatives -> response measure -> flip condition -> ADR), and a module that quietly grew into it would have made that decision by accretion. Four mechanisms, each against a measured failure: (1) an explicit `NODE_OPTIONS` heap cap emitted by the GENERATORS and declared in `.claude/settings.json`, never in a paste; (2) a seat retired and re-booted from its render after 8 hours or 6 merges, **which inverts the standing long-seat practice and says so**; (3) a teardown that kills the whole process TREE and reports its survivors rather than assuming the kill reached them; (4) a transcript size bound plus a streaming read, so `scripts/lane_cost.py` -- the instrument every routing decision now depends on -- stops materialising whole transcripts. All five upstream §1 findings land in `ecosystem/quality-requirements.yaml`, three as `measured` with neutered-organ trips and two as `candidate` under the register's own honesty rule. - Done when: `scripts/resource_lifecycle.py` exists with the four mechanisms and one home per constant; a launch surface with no cap, or a cap at the wrong value, is REFUSED; a spawned grandchild is reaped by teardown, witnessed by a test that reproduces the SURVIVAL first; `lane_cost` streams and refuses an oversize transcript; and the five findings carry their evidence pointers - refs `docs/audits/2026-09-15-technical-batch-aa-manifest.md` (the batch that dispatched it), `ecosystem/quality-requirements.yaml` (QR-RES-002/003/004, QR-AVAIL-009, QR-PERF-005), `tests/test_resource_lifecycle.py` and `tests/test_quality_requirement_trips.py` (the proof layer), `scripts/resource_lifecycle.py` (the organ), `scripts/lane_cost.py` (the streaming reader), `scripts/gen_lane_contract.py` and `scripts/gen_seat_boot.py` (the two generators that render the cap and the lifetime), and `.claude/settings.json` (the env block that delivers the cap) - **two OPEN ITEMS for the integrator, neither decidable in a lane:** (a) the five UPSTREAM entries carry their evidence as a described report rather than an issue NUMBER, because the digest that carried them records content and not numbers and inventing one would be a fabricated citation dressed as verification;
