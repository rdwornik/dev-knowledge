- [#726] [P1][S] **A session whose worktree was torn down silently resolves to the PRIMARY checkout, so its next write lands on the integrator's branch** - AX8-5 of `to-cc/AMEND-BATCH-X-ROSTER-008.md`, FILE and RED-first, carried at `docs/intake/2026-09-11-tech-batch-x-roster.md` (intake 93) Part 8. **Witnessed on the dispatcher session, 2026-09-11.** · **The failure is silent and it aims at the worst possible target.** When a worktree is removed, a session still sitting in it does not error -- its cwd resolves upward to the PRIMARY checkout, and the primary is where the integrator works. So a stale lane session's next write does not go nowhere; it goes onto the integration branch, at exactly the moment the integrator is merging. The blast radius is the opposite of what intuition suggests: teardown feels like the safe end of a lane's life. · **Every protection this repo has is keyed to being IN a worktree**, which is why nothing catches it. Worktree isolation, the lane branch, the one-checkout-one-session rule -- all of them assume the worktree exists. A session whose worktree is gone has quietly become an unisolated writer on `main`'s checkout while still believing it is a lane, and `git status` in it looks entirely normal. · **Two defences, and AX8-5 names both because either alone leaves a hole.** (1) TEARDOWN must end or refuse sessions on that worktree -- closing the door at the moment of removal. (2) The Stop hook must detect cwd-not-a-worktree and REFUSE writes -- catching the session that survived anyway, including one torn down by something other than the sanctioned teardown path. · **`[#527]` and `block-commit-on-main` are adjacent and do not cover this.** A commit refusal on `main` catches a commit; it does not catch a WRITE to the working tree, which is the act that corrupts the integrator's checkout mid-merge and needs no commit to do damage. · Done when: teardown ends or refuses sessions whose cwd is the worktree being removed; the Stop hook detects a cwd that is not a worktree and REFUSES writes rather than warning; and a RED-first test reproduces the witnessed failure -- a session whose worktree is removed resolving to the primary checkout -- and FAILS before the guard makes it green · **Done-when carried from AX8-5's two named defences**, with the RED-first requirement AX8-5 states · refs `docs/intake/2026-09-11-tech-batch-x-roster.md` (intake 93, Part 8 AX8-5), `to-cc/AMEND-BATCH-X-ROSTER-008.md` AX8-5, `.claude/rules/git-discipline.md` (WORKTREE TEARDOWN IS TWO BRANCHES -- the teardown path this must join), `scripts/session_end_backpressure.py` (the Stop hook), `[#527]` (the anti-direct-to-main mechanism -- adjacent, catches commits not writes), `[#405]` (nothing verifies no leftovers at session end), `[#429]` (worktree provisioning portability), `[#512]` (an open-batch refusal that does not scrub the git dir) · kill-candidates: none -- `[#527]` owns refusing a COMMIT on main and this row owns a WRITE from a session that no longer knows where it is; `[#405]` owns detecting leftovers the session created rather than the session itself becoming one · source: `to-cc/AMEND-BATCH-X-ROSTER-008.md` AX8-5, filed by lane `lane-x-000-batch-x-roster-lands`
