- [#884] [P1][M] **An unbounded research fan-out ran 17+ hours to re-answer a settled question -- a research dispatch states its agent cap, per-agent deadline and token budget before launch, and a step past its deadline is abandoned with what it has** - Operator order 2026-09-17, from a measured loss. One research session consumed ~0.5M tokens over 17+ hours; one of its read steps ran 5h26m and had not returned when the session was killed; two of its subagents ran 12-17 hours each. It was answering a question already answered: the prose-as-source digest (`DIGEST-2026-09-16-prose-as-source.md`, ingested at `b9ceb000`) was written a day earlier and its headline numbers were already in use. Three defects, none with a row before this one: **(1) no bound on a fan-out** -- a research dispatch carries no agent cap and no per-agent deadline, so an unbounded sweep can consume a day and a budget with nothing to show; **(2) "stop" is not deliverable** -- a session inside a long tool call cannot receive an instruction until the call returns, so the session that most needs the order is the one that cannot hear it, and the only remedy today is killing it, which loses whatever it held; **(3) nothing prices a question before launch** -- cost is discovered afterwards. This is the same class as the hook wedges (`[#808]`, `[#863]`): an operation with no bound looks identical to work. It is the **browser seat's** defect: across this window it launched nine, then five, then ten subagents and never once stated when they should stop. · Done when: (1) every dispatched research task states, up front and in the dispatch itself, its agent cap, its per-agent deadline and its token budget, and a dispatch missing any of the three is refused rather than launched; (2) a step or subagent that exceeds its deadline is abandoned and returns what it has so far -- it is not waited on, and its partial result is kept, not lost with the session; (3) a stop order reaches a session without killing it -- or, where the runtime cannot deliver one mid-call, the per-call deadline is the delivery, and that limit is recorded, not implied away; (4) before launch the question is checked against the existing digests and audits, and a question already answered is answered from the artifact instead of re-researched; (5) the launch records its stated budget and the close records the spend against it, so cost is known before and measured after · refs `docs/intake/2026-09-16-tech-browser-seat-findings-off-the-transport.md` (digest roster, `b9ceb000`), `[#808]`, `[#863]`, `[#883]` · kill-candidates: none -- `[#808]`/`[#863]` bound a hook's time, `[#883]` prices a gate's worth; this row bounds a research dispatch · source: operator order 2026-09-17
