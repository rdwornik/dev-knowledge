- [#792] [P1][L] **The runtime-resource lifecycle organ: two regimes, two mechanisms, no shared thresholds** — the first increment of intake 101 (`[#791]` carries the decision; this row carries the mechanisms, and **neither closes the other**). **`QR-RES-001` records that the least discretionary quality attribute in the register — "automatic arithmetic, not a judgement call" — is the one with no organ at all**, and this row is that organ. It owns `scripts/resource_lifecycle.py` (the LOCAL regime: the allocation refusal, the retirement predicate, the process-TREE teardown, the RSS sampler) and `scripts/context_reclamation.py` (the checkpoint clear and its resume-from-files trip test), with witnesses in `tests/test_resource_lifecycle.py` and `tests/test_context_reclamation.py`. · **THE TWO FILES ARE TWO FILES ON PURPOSE, and collapsing them is the defect.** Measured 2026-09-15 and recorded in `docs/audits/2026-09-15-technical-lane-aa-14-resource-lifecycle.md` §2: per-turn COST is driven by accumulated TURN COUNT (0.701 → 1.247 × own median across turn-index bands, and 0.915 → 1.003 across an eight-fold elapsed spread once turn index is HELD), while process MEMORY is driven by session AGE (255.6 MB under 0.5 h → 383.0 MB at 0.5–1 h, r = 0.440 and 21.4 MB/h under 4.5 h). **Two quantities, two drivers, two mechanisms — clearing context does not free process memory, and retiring a session to reclaim cost throws away a cache that was not the problem.** Building one mechanism for both is why neither works today. · **AND THE TWO REGIMES ARE NOT ONE RESOURCE.** Local is a fixed ceiling allocated against — 27.67 GB total, 13.52 GB non-Claude, 3 GB reserve, 413.3 MB per seat (287.0 heavy + 126.3 light, measured as an exact 31/31 process split), giving a **computed** 27-seat ceiling that must be re-read rather than typed, because it moves whenever the non-Claude load does. Cloud is a meter run down — per-minute uptime, an idle timeout, and a container build. **A single threshold tuned for one is wrong for the other**, and the register must never carry a figure that spans them. The cloud half is `scripts/codespace_regime.py`, witnessed by `tests/test_codespace_regime.py`, and it is a THIRD file for the same reason the first two are two: it carries no memory figure and can never acquire one, which the witnesses assert as a pairwise-disjoint-dimension property across all three modules rather than leaving it to a comment. Its measured figures come from ONE probe container, `lane-aa-14-probe` — 11.97 metered minutes, the whole cost of the measurement — whose receipt is the first row of `logs/CODESPACE-RECEIPTS.jsonl`. **The receipt REFUSES to be written without uptime**: tokens alone price the wrong resource here, because an attached idle container burns budget while its token count stays flat, so an idle lane reads as a cheap one. **The prebuild question is answered with a RECOMMENDATION and an explicit `decidable = False`**, not a ruling: the time side is measured (155.8 s cold vs 34.1 s prebuilt, saving 4.1 min/week at our measured 2.0 creations/week, so *disable*), the money side returns 404 on this account, and the flip condition is stated at 7 creations/week so the recommendation is not an opinion. · **What is DERIVED and what is NOT, stated so a later reader does not promote a judgement into a measurement.** Derived: the reclamation trigger at **1.30 × the seat's own running median sustained 5 turns** (from the fire-rate table over 681 sessions — 1.10 fires in 84.4%, 1.30 in 52.4%, 2.00 in 0.9%); the lifetime bound at **4 hours** (the last band where the RSS evidence is more than three processes). **NOT derived, and reported so:** the RSS threshold is **not computable from transcripts** — no record type carries any memory field, and the contract's prescribed proxy is dead, with per-turn latency FLAT against own median from 0–15 min to 480+ min across 92,743 turns. The RSS leg therefore ships against the cross-sectional ~385 MB plateau with that provenance recorded, and `resource_lifecycle.py sample` exists to replace it with a longitudinal figure once it has run for a while. **A sampler with no history supports no threshold, and none is claimed.** · **RED-first where testable, and the teardown witness is the one that matters.** A test that checks the parent died proves nothing — that is the behaviour we already have. The witness is a spawned GRANDCHILD that survives a naive parent kill and is reaped by the tree teardown, written to fail first. Upstream confirms reparenting and the requested fix is tree teardown; we independently reproduced it (a 46.8-hour orphan, and a grandchild that outlived its parent's kill by ~8 minutes — `QR-AVAIL-005`). · **The allocation refusal is a REFUSAL, not a report.** `scripts/seat_refusals.py` carries `lane-ceiling`, which is ADR-110's 4–6 lane COUNT and reads no memory; `--check-worktrees` reads the live worktree list, also a count. **Measured at filing time: 31 live seats against a 27-seat ceiling and 1.62 GB free against a 3.00 GB reserve — the box was over on both legs and nothing on it noticed.** · **THE TRANSCRIPT READER IS BOUNDED, and the bound is streaming rather than a refusal.** `scripts/lane_cost.py` read each transcript with `read_text()` then `.splitlines()` — the whole file as a str AND as a list of str at once — inside a module `scripts/fleet_health.py` imports and therefore runs at SESSION START, on the box whose memory is the constraint this row exists to manage. Measured 2026-09-15: 3,108 transcripts under `~/.claude/projects`, 2.55 GB total, median 0.34 MB, largest **29.56 MB** — about 60 MB of peak for that one file. It now streams, so peak is bounded by the longest LINE (1.360 MB measured across the 20 largest), and `MAX_TRANSCRIPT_LINE_BYTES` sits at 8 MiB, ~6× the largest real line, so what it catches is corruption rather than size. **“Skip any transcript over N MB” would also be a size bound and it would silently drop a lane’s whole spend** — the number that means “not measured” becoming the number that means “free”, which is the class `lane_cost` already refuses for an unpriced model. Streaming counts every line; only a line past the bound is dropped, and that drop WARNS. · **The lane’s own hooks are DECLARED in the same act as their wiring.** `ecosystem/parity-surfaces.yaml` gains `resource_lifecycle.py` and `codespace_regime.py` in the local-owned hook map, because a SessionStart hook wired without a declaration is WARN-undeclared to `fleet_parity` — a hook declared a batch later is undeclared for a batch. `conductor.py` and `logs_retention.py` are left exactly as found: both already wired on main and already WARN-undeclared there, and absorbing another row’s debt into this diff would hide when it was paid and by whom. `tests/test_audit.py::test_check_fleet_parity_green_on_live_repo` is **RED on main before this lane** on those two plus two child-repo `override.md` findings, and is left red at exactly those four. · **THE FIVE UPSTREAM CITATIONS ARE SUPPLIED, and each issue body was opened and read before being cited** — `#22968` session degradation over ~30 min (CLOSED, not planned), `#43944` Bash-tool processes reparented to PID 1, asking for full-process-tree cleanup (CLOSED, not planned), `#18859` four idle sessions at ~15 GB over ~18 h with context constant at 6,664 chars (**OPEN** — the only one of the five), `#21022` a 102 MB / 8,366-line transcript at 90% RAM (CLOSED, not planned), `#56693` the `--max-old-space-size` cap that prevents the crash without fixing the leak (CLOSED, not planned). They were filed OWED because the digest this lane was given carried five reports and no numbers; the gap is now closed by citation rather than by a guess. **Four of five being CLOSED AS NOT PLANNED changes what we build**: a workaround whose retirement condition is “when upstream fixes it” has no trigger to wait for, so `resource_lifecycle.teardown` is load-bearing indefinitely rather than temporarily. · **AND THE REPORTS ARE NOT OUR MEASUREMENTS — recorded as a rule, not as a remark.** The contract carried “~15 GB idle growth” as a fact about this box; it is `#18859`’s reporter on a 24 GB macOS host, and the largest process here measured **393.9 MB, ~38× smaller**. A threshold set against the reported magnitude would have refused nothing here while appearing fully armed. `QR-RES-006` now carries the rule as case law and `LESSONS.md` carries it as a forward rule: an upstream report is recorded AS A REPORT with its source, its figures stay the reporter’s, and every threshold this repo enforces rests only on a measurement taken here. What travels is the DIRECTION and the MECHANISM — `#18859`’s constant context while memory grew is this row’s two-mechanism thesis, and that half reproduced exactly. · **The falsified proxy is STRUCK, not deleted.** Per-turn latency against own median cannot detect session degradation — flat to three decimals across every age band over 92,743 turns — and `QR-PERF-006`’s `metric:` now says so in place, because a proxy quietly removed is re-proposed by the next person who reasons their way to it, which is how it was proposed this time. The RSS threshold is correspondingly split into its two honest halves: **UNDERIVABLE** (no memory field on any of 15 transcript record types — a property of the data, not of the derivation) and then **CHOSEN** (385 MB, basis: the cross-sectional plateau of 31 live processes, replaced once `sample` has longitudinal history). · **WHAT THE CODESPACE ANSWER UNBLOCKS:** the substrate is LIVE, so the lanes deferred on “substrate dead” are dispatchable again — a dispatch decision for the operator, explicitly NOT tonight, and not this lane’s to make. · **Proposed, deliberately not applied:** the `NODE_OPTIONS` heap cap belongs in the GENERATOR's launch line and never in a paste, but `scripts/gen_seat_boot.py` is `[#789]`'s sole-owned file and `[#789]` is rewriting that exact render surface.
