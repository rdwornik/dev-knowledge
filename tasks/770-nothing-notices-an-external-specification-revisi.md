Its warning is explicit and is the item this row carries: *"warto tak naprawdę już o tym wiedzieć i nie projektować serwerów MCP, wykorzystujących te zdeprecjonowane technologie, bo będzie to znaczyło konieczność ich całkowitej przebudowy"* (`AJ_M04_transkrypcje.md:876-879`, verified) -- know this now and do not design servers on the deprecated features, because it will mean rebuilding them completely. · **We have two currency organs and neither watches an outside standard.** `coherence_nudge` (`79ff7dd5`) fires when a spec registered in `_SPEC_REGISTRY` changes without a version bump, and `check-against-spec` (`89f71402`) reconciles each dependent site -- both are about a spec THIS REPO OWNS moving under its own dependents. `[#385]` and `[#495]` distribute DEPENDENCY VERSION bumps through the apply channel, landing in `ecosystem/deployed-versions.yaml`. An external specification that revises on its own cadence is in neither population. · **The exposure is concrete and already scheduled.** `[#729]` commits to building a minimal stdio MCP server.
