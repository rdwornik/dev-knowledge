- [#737] [P2][S] **A SEAT-BOOT render cannot express the seat's own model, so the operator's re-tiering ruling has nowhere to live but prose the next seat never reads** - AX22-3 of `to-cc/AMEND-BATCH-X-ROSTER-022.md`, carried at `docs/intake/2026-09-11-tech-batch-x-roster.md` (intake 93). · **The defect is structural and was measured, not inferred:** `scripts/gen_seat_boot.py` contained **no model concept at all** — a grep for `model` over the module returned nothing — so the five renders it produces could state a seat's refusals, its route, its stop condition and its provenance, but not the tier it runs at. A ruling about seat models therefore had exactly two homes: an AMEND file the next seat is not required to read, or a sentence in a handoff. Both are the "decided, scheduled nowhere" class that `[#692]` exists to refuse, arriving at the one artifact whose entire purpose is to tell a booting seat what it needs before it can act. · **Why it is a row even though the field now exists.** The field was added under this row's own number by the dispatcher seat on 2026-09-11 (`SEAT_MODELS` plus a `model:` field in the render header, RED-first: three tests failing on the missing attribute before the code existed, suite 28 passed). What is NOT yet witnessed is the part that makes the ruling true rather than merely recorded: that each seat actually BOOTS at its stated tier, and that a fresh bundle cut reconciles P12. A field nobody boots from is the same inventory-not-harness class in a smaller package. · **The P12 consequence is known and is not a defect.** A fresh render now carries `model:`; the frozen `docs/handoffs/2026-09-10-dev-knowledge-architect/` bundle predates the ruling and does not, so `gen_seat_boot.py verify --bundle` reports DRIFT 5 of 5 against it. That bundle was deliberately NOT regenerated: handoff bundles are immutable (`CLAUDE.md` critical rule 3). P12 is wired into no gate — it is absent from `.pre-commit-config.yaml` — so nothing is blocked, and the next bundle cut renders the field and passes. Recorded so the next seat reads a known consequence rather than discovering an alarming one. · **Placement of the field is load-bearing:** `model:` sits between `date:` and `ch8-sha256:` because `_HEADER_RE` stops at the pipe following `date`, making the field purely additive — no existing reader changes.
