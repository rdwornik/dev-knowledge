# 07 · Your turn to ask

You've proven comprehension. Before you start work, you may ask **up to 3 questions**
— the things that, if you got them wrong, would send you down the wrong path. Rob
answers from memory or the sender chat.

Good questions are load-bearing and specific:

- A fact you'd otherwise have to *assume* (and assuming would be costly).
- A scope/priority call where `05_NOW` left genuine ambiguity (e.g. the doc-truth
  sweep vs enforcement-layer sequencing — which does Rob want first this session?).
- A boundary you're unsure applies to your planned first move.

Do **not** ask:

- Anything answered in `01`–`05` or verifiable yourself with a quick tool call
  (verify inline — that's the back-and-forth rule).
- Open-ended "what should I do?" — propose a reasoned plan instead and ask Rob to
  confirm or redirect.

Write your ≤3 questions, then wait for Rob's answers before beginning.
