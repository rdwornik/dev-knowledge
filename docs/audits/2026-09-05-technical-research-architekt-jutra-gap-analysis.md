## §0 — The result, in two sentences

**They built a harness that can see itself and cannot enforce itself.** Maister is 48 components (6 commands,
17 skills, 25 agents) that decompose one feature into up to 14 gated phases inside a single run, instrument
the loop with real traces and per-token cost accounting, and then rely almost entirely on *prose* to make the
model honour its own gates — the plugin ships a `SessionStart` hook whose whole job is re-injecting the rule
"actually stop at gates," and a second hook to re-inject it again after compaction.

**We built a harness that enforces itself rigorously and instruments its governance but not its model calls.**
`.dev-knowledge` is the **22 gates** counted at `ecosystem/doc-counts.md:15@6de676fb` — a generated surface,
regenerated by `gen_doc_counts.py` and checked by the `doc_claims` audit check, not a number typed into prose.
They split 18 pre-commit + 2 commit-msg + 2 pre-push (rostered `CLAUDE.md:150-180@6de676fb`), two of them
failing closed — plus immutability, append-only records, a byte-capped boot contract and a four-outcome
decision funnel, governing work decomposed *between* sessions into lanes and batches. It times every audit
check (`scripts/audit.py:5215-5230@6de676fb`) and freezes real session transcripts as committed fixtures. What
it does **not** have is per-LLM-call cost and token tracing: `scripts/cost_usage_telemetry.py:1-30@6de676fb` is
*"Library only; no call sites"*.

**The first draft of this sentence said "cannot see itself," and an adversarial review proved that false.**
The two systems are complements at the level of *enforcement* — that half is solid, and their own repo proves
it. They are not complements at the level of *observability*: we see more of ourselves than I first claimed.

**The most transferable result of this arc is a methodology finding, not a gap.** Every absence claim in the
first draft was searched in **their** vocabulary. Our words are *supplement*, *asks register*,
*lived-workflow*, *freeze*, *legibility*, *shape*, *trends* — and none of them was searched for. Three of eight
ONLY-AJ rows were consequently false, and five more were overstated; an adversarial pass that searched in *our*
vocabulary falsified them in eighteen minutes. **Searching a second system for the first system's words
manufactures absence.** §6 records the whole tally; the rows below are the corrected set.

**Extraction.** 27 text files: 13 per-lesson transcripts split from the two consolidated `.md` sources at

**Skipped, and why:**
- **All 13 MP3s — not transcribed.** Operator ruling at P0. Transcript coverage is 1:1 by filename stem
  (13 MP3 / 13 `_transkrypcja.pdf`), so transcription bought no coverage. The only thing it would have
  bought is timestamps, and the corpus has none anywhere.
- **Six of eight zips — not unpacked.** Byte-identical re-bundles of the loose files, SHA-256 spot-checked
  on two samples. Only the two `.md.zip` files carried unique content and those were unpacked.
- **`AJ_M02L07` has no slide deck** — 12 decks for 13 lessons. Its transcript is the sole source for it.

**Two findings from P0 that shaped everything after:**
1. **`pdftotext` silently drops Polish diacritics without `-enc UTF-8`.** Not an error — every combining
   mark vanishes, the word stem survives, and the output reads as a typo rather than as corruption. Caught
   by diffing extraction against the clean `.md`. The RED condition for P1 was a per-file diacritic assertion;
   0 of 14 PDF-derived files were RED after the fix.
2. **The contract's locator grammar was unobtainable.** It froze "page / mm:ss"; `grep` for `mm:ss` returns
   **zero** across all 15 transcript sources. The operator ratified a substitute: transcripts cite
   `<lesson-slug>:<line>` against the canonical `.md`, PDFs cite `p<N>`. The P2 RED rule held unchanged
   against that grammar.

**Closure witness.** 27 sheets == 27 extracted files. **970 claims, 0 without a locator, 0 carrying
comparison language** — verified mechanically by a section-aware parser over the `## CLAIMS` block of every
sheet, not by asking the agents whether they complied.

## §4 — What we have that they do not (so we do not cargo-cult)

The single most important asymmetry: **their gates are prose and ours are mechanisms.**

Maister's own shipped repo contains the proof, and it is a single pair of lines. In
`architekt-jutra-code`, the task `2026-03-28-microkernel-skeleton-app` carries a verification report whose
verdict is **`Deployment Decision: NO-GO`** (`verification/reality-check.md:5`) — and that same task's
`orchestrator-state.yml:32` records **`status: completed`**. The verdict flipped no machine-readable field,
because nothing connects the two. The whole `verification/` family — severity tables, Critical/Warning/Info
counts, `Fixable: true/false`, GO/NO-GO verdicts — *reads* like a gate and *is* a report.

The plugin corroborates it. Exactly **one** hook in the entire plugin can veto an action
(`hooks/block-destructive-commands.sh:1-42`); the other two only inject text. Every phase gate, every
verification threshold (100 % / 95-99 % / <95 %, `agents/test-suite-runner.md:99-104`), every retry cap and
every "SELF-CHECK" line is an instruction the model is asked to apply to itself, with nothing external
checking. The plugin visibly does not trust this: it ships a `SessionStart` hook
(`hooks/skill-invocation-reminder.sh:4-9`) whose entire purpose is to re-inject "actually call
`AskUserQuestion` at gates" into every new session, and a second hook to re-inject it again after
compaction, because compaction is a known point where it gets dropped. That is a system compensating for an
unenforced rule by repeating it louder.

Ours that they have no analogue for, each verified at `@6de676fb`:

- **The decision funnel** — every audit finding is triaged into exactly one of OWNED / DISCHARGED /
  CANDIDATE / REJECTED (`CLAUDE.md:75`). AJ has no concept of what happens to a finding after it is found.
- **Immutability with amendment discipline** — ADRs, transcripts, handoffs and audits are immutable;
  supersede, never edit (`CLAUDE.md:91`). Their artifacts are regenerated.
- **Append-only institutional record** — `LESSONS.md`, `logs/TOKEN-LOG.md`, newest-first `JOURNAL.md`
  (`CLAUDE.md:68`).
- **Gates that fail closed** — `block-unanchored-push` refuses a push to `main` whose range carries no
  JOURNAL anchor (`CLAUDE.md:179`).
- **A byte-capped, test-gated boot contract** — ≤ 24,576 B, enforced by `tests/test_claude_md_byte_cap.py`
  (`CLAUDE.md:14`). AJ observes that `CLAUDE.md` bloats (`M01L03:52-55`) and stops at the observation.
- **"Never restate a count or roster in prose — cite the surface that computes it"** (`CLAUDE.md:70`).
- **"Resolve a locator before you act on it"** (`CLAUDE.md:71`).
- **Layer-2 invariant** — validators may validate, never orchestrate (`CLAUDE.md:93`). Their harness
  orchestrates freely; the constraint has no counterpart there.
- **A cross-session handoff contract with a probe gate** — the forced primary-source read and its probe manifest, `protocols/HANDOFF_PROCESS.md:213-244@6de676fb`. Their
  continuity is within a task; there is no session-to-session transfer contract.
- **A two-tier adoption bar** (`docs/decisions/ADR-112-two-tier-adoption-bar.md@6de676fb`). AJ *asks* "ready-made harness or build your own?"
  (`M01L01:119-123`) and leaves it open; we have a ratified answer shape.
- **A six-stage continuous-improvement pipeline** — Discovery → Triage → Evaluation → Decision →
  Implementation → Review (`protocols/PLAYBOOK.md:3633-3644@6de676fb` — `:3603` is only the chapter heading). They assign reflection homework.
- **A fan-out bound with a recorded scar** — `ecosystem/routing-table.yaml:43-45` binds the `fan_out` role

### §4.1 — The residue hypothesis, tested (operator ruling 5)

The ruling asked whether `aj-code` keeping its harness's session residue in-repo is the same idea as our
`logs/` TRACE lane and `docs/handoffs/`, or ONLY-AJ. Tested against the repo, the answer is **both, and the
distinction they lack is the interesting part.**

**Deliberate, and stated:** `aj-code:README.md:24-34` — *"All workflow artifacts are committed in `.maister/tasks/`
so you can review the full AI-assisted development process."* Fifteen task directories carry per-task state
(`orchestrator-state.yml`), plans, specs, work logs and verification reports. **No raw conversation
transcripts** live there — the closest thing in the whole repo is `agent/trajectory.json`, and it sits under
`tools/kg-incidents/jobs/`, not under `.maister/`.

**Read back — by instruction, not by mechanism.** Their root `aj-code:CLAUDE.md:3` says *"Read @.maister/docs/INDEX.md
before starting any task"*; later phases cite earlier ones by path, and a development task's
`orchestrator-state.yml:47-49` points at its sibling research task. But **no script anywhere parses
`.maister/tasks/**` or `orchestrator-state.yml`** — the only non-model reader is `dashboard-data.js`, a
one-time generated snapshot. The read-back is the model obeying prose.

**Incidental, and undistinguished.** Alongside the deliberate corpus sit 23 Playwright-MCP browser console
logs and page snapshots (landed inside commit `ba41674 "Module 03 Implementation"` next to real source
changes), a session lock file carrying a live `sessionId` and `pid`
(`tools/kg-incidents/.claude/scheduled_tasks.lock`), and an empty `.opik-tracing-enabled` flag. None is
gitignored; none is documented.

**Verdict: same idea, weaker boundary.** Our equivalent — handoff bundles, audits, `JOURNAL.md` — is
*generated to a template, seal-checked at commit (`check-seal-identity`), and immutable once landed*, so the
deliberate record cannot blur into droppings. Theirs has no such seam, and the repo shows exactly what that
costs. The one place they *do* draw the line is the eval archive (§3 row 21), and there they draw it better
than we do: an explicit un-ignore plus a written strip list.
