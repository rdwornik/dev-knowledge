The layer matrix

click a layer to isolate it

This is the finding that makes a render possible at all. 11,515 edges collapse into 12 aggregate cells across seven layers — 17 distinct (source, target, kind) triples. A node-level force graph of 1,783 nodes is a hairball nobody reads; the aggregate fits in one glance and still shows every pathology.

Edge width is log-scaled because the range is 1 to 5,898 — linear width would render eleven of the twelve cells as invisible hairlines, which is the same failure as omitting them. Self-edges (a layer citing itself) are drawn as loops and counted in the totals.

Where the graph is blind

governed / tracked

A path is governed only if one of the five inputs names it. Three whole directories sit at zero — drawn hatched, because a 0% bar and a missing bar look identical and mean opposite things.

One honest divergence, stated rather than smoothed. DM-3 reports tasks/ at 379/382; this measures 24/387. Neither is wrong — DM-3 counted NODE_TASK vertices, this counts NODE_FILE vertices only, and a task row is both a file on disk and a task node. The overall figure moves with it: 50.2% their way, 37.7% this way. The measure has to be named or the number means nothing.

The sharpest fact in the graph

The L0 layer contains exactly one node: .claude/commands/override.md. It is present only because the deploy manifest declares a component that ships it — and /override was retired by ADR-85's 2026-08-03 amendment. Seventeen of eighteen tracked .claude/ files are invisible to the graph.

The single L0 edge in the entire corpus therefore points at a retired command. The layer exists by accident of a manifest entry, not by design.

II · the funnel

Funnel state machine

live counts

ADR-111 routes every finding into exactly one disposition. Intake carries a seven-state status enum whose three terminal states (dashed) relocate to archive/ byte-identically.

READY 19 is the pressure point. It is the largest non-terminal stage and the one with a ruled 30-day threshold behind it; ACCEPTED 19 is standing authority that deliberately stays visible rather than archiving.

Two funnels, two corpora, and they do not reconcile — by construction. funnel_coverage reports corpus 843 / dispositioned 78 / uncovered 763 over the audit corpus. The session banner reports 285 over a different population. Neither is the intake enum above. Three surfaces say “funnel” and count three different things; ATLAS renders each against its own denominator rather than summing them into a number that means nothing.

Named absence — per-lane cost is underivable today

The only cost surface in the repo is logs/TOKEN-LOG.md, and it cannot attribute a lane. It is a weekly host-wide ccusage aggregate: dollars, token totals and per-model percentages for the whole machine, across every repo and every session.

Its newest entry is 2026-08-04. Batches D, E and F ran 2026-08-29 through 09-01. All three fall entirely outside the log's coverage — a 25-day gap before batch D even began.

And the registry says so itself. ecosystem/provider-registry.yaml declares in its own header: “ROUTING IS NOT HERE … This registry records model IDENTITY, not which model gets routed to which job.” The routing table lives at ~/.claude/ROUTING.md, at L0, outside this repository.

The enabling row already exists and is open. [#615] — a model+version commit trailer, hook-enforced at commit-msg — is what makes this series derivable. DB-1 already rendered per-model quality as ABSENT for the same reason and did not proxy it. This panel holds that line: a partial cost series is worse than an absent one, because it looks whole.

The cheapest dependency act is the one you do not perform.
