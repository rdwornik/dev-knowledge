## 2. Use cases — what happens now

**a. "I paste one order at night and go to sleep."** The dispatcher launches every lane through one
launcher under a memory cap, repairs from the integrator's REFUSED orders, and nudges stuck lanes;
the integrator merges one path, tears down, and writes a digest with a MORNING section of every
decision the lanes took. Evidence: wave 5A, 10 merges, 0 waits. **Missing:** finished sessions stay
resident and eat memory; the handback line is not enforced at write time, so two handbacks were seen
2-4 h late.

**b. "A lane's work reaches main."** Split attribution, each check once, CI paired by node id, local
verdict authoritative. Evidence: 55.5 min median, 26 min after selection. **Missing:** CI as the
gate; the per-batch registry takes 3 h 40 min and launders regressions (fixed by the pending
one-registry lane); the documentation tier once per batch.

**c. "What happened while I slept?"** The ledger, the digest, the plan file and the rows. **Missing:**
the ledger regenerated by the batch close, not by the architect.

**d. "A new architect window opens."** The boot points to where things live; the paste is gated at
20 KB; probes run for real. Evidence: merged 10b1570e. **Missing:** today's cut is the first proof;
the RATIFICATION probe expects the code's path, not the schema's.

**e. "A big design decision."** ADR + matrix + debate + independent check, all as records. Evidence:
ADR-121, its three audit records, ai-council operated from its front door in 2.5 min.
**Missing:** the debate organ on the router; ai-council's fate.

**f. "Copilot produces a routine lane."** Admitted in the registry; three attempts failed on
infrastructure (a reaped dispatcher shell last night), none on quality. **Missing:** a producer that
runs as its own session; the launcher asking the router (row [#691] Half B).

**g. "The harness runs off my laptop."** Not yet. Known precisely: 0 hard-coded Drive paths, 119 read
sites; two PowerShell hooks are the only hard break; the devcontainer lacks codex, agy, copilot and
pwsh; Codespaces are capped at 4 cores; the recommendation is one persistent Linux VM.

**h. "A real monorepo feature goes through the loop."** Not yet. This is the finish line.

## 4. What the three days cost, and where it went

About 45 lanes, 26 merges over five nights, dozens of audits. The waste was rarely in the code: it
was time lost to prose — routing tables in orders that sessions ignored, contracts in a grammar the
lint could not read, handback lines nobody validated — and to memory on one laptop.

## 5. Lessons that became doctrine

- The backbone is code; prose states intent and checks the code.
- Two sources of truth is the recurring root cause: registries, merge paths, transport, baselines.
- One choke point per act: one launcher, one comparator, one writer, one path layer.
- Measure in real sessions, never in isolation; a census zero is never evidence alone.
- Prior art before design: the router, the deny-and-point guard and the closure organ all existed.
- The PLAYBOOK is for the operator; Claude acts on code, data and rulings.
