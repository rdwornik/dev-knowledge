# SEED -- three D0 rows whose only authority was the batch order

## Problem / motivation

Eighteen rows, [#1625] to [#1642], were born on 2026-10-18 from BATCH-D0-PROCESS-2026-10-18 section 0.4 without a
ratifying act named in their bodies. ADR-111 section 4 makes a birth auditable only if `decided-by` names an
ADR or a recorded operator ruling. Lane e1-rows put a landed ruling under fifteen of them (R98 to R136, see its
JOURNAL entry). Three have none: the operator's GO covered the batch plan, and no ratified ruling, no Accepted
ADR and no ACCEPTED intake names their subject. Those three leave the backlog and wait here for a ratifying act.

The search that found no ruling: `row_close`, `STATUS.md`, `MAP-DISPATCH` and a relaunch of a merged lane were
looked up in `protocols/STANDING_RULINGS.md` (through R136), in every `to-browser/RATIFICATION-*.md` and in
`docs/decisions/ADR-*.md`; none appears in a ruling or an ADR.

## Scenarios (+1 view)

- A merge lands and its rows are closed by hand after the claim is released, because `scripts/row_close.py` is
  wired into no template, command or moment (the first item).
- A browser seat boots and finds no single front door: the transport INDEX omits most live reports and the
  STATUS file the plan defines is generated by nothing (the second item).
- A queue run over a finished batch relaunches a lane whose receipt already reads MERGED (the third item).

- **Should:** the three be ruled together with the adoption audit's candidates, one funnel, not one by one.
- **Could:** none.
